#5A. Pedal Adapters




## Hall-Effect Adapter

Harcroft's Hall-Effect Adapter is used to convert the logarithmic signals to linear.
This works with Thrustmaster pedals and GoKart pedals sold on Aliexpress

Components
* 1 x MCP6004-I/P-ND OP-AMP
* 6 x 1N4934 Diodes
* 1 x 1uf Capacitor
* 3 x 10K potentiometers
* 3 x 100 kΩ Resistors
* **If being to adapt the Thrustmaster pedals:** you will need a female RJ-12 cable and follow the diagram below
* **If being to adapt the GoKart pedals:** you can wire directly to the circuit
* **If being used for the original controller:** you will need the Male Mini 6DIN connector
* **If being used for PC:** An microcontroller will be needed to convert the signals to USB
