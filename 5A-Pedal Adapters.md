## Hall-Effect Adapter

Harcroft's Hall-Effect Adapter is used to convert the logarithmic signals to linear.
This works with Thrustmaster pedals and GoKart pedals sold on Aliexpress. Harcroft sells both versions of this adaoter but you can also make it yourself.

Components
* 1 x MCP6004-I/P-ND OP-AMP
* 6 x 1N4934 Diodes
* 1 x 1uf Capacitor
* 3 x 10K potentiometers
* 3 x 100 kΩ Resistors

* **If adapting the Thrustmaster pedals:** you will need a female RJ-12 cable and follow the wiring.
* **If adapting the dapt the GoKart pedals:** you can wire directly to the circuit

* **If being used for the original controller:** you will need the Male Mini 6DIN connector
* **If being used for PC:** An microcontroller will be needed to convert the signals to USB. Thrustmaster also has an official adapter for sale
