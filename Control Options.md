- Mapping all toggles to one button/switch.

## Ergonomics
- Moving Camera Zoom In/Out, Night Scope, and Line Color Change to Left Block. This puts all main camera all camera controls in one place, the Zoom in/out is usually done as a rocker trigger on the left stick
- Moving F.S.S., Main Weapon Control, Sub Weapon Control, Reload, and Chaff to right block. This puts all combat controls in one place.
- Mapping a joystick's throttle to acceleration, similar to how it is configured in Mechwarrior
