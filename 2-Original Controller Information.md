## Overview
The Steel Battalion Controller, also nicknamed the Mega Jockey 9000 and sometimes the VT Controller, is a large four piece videogame controller built solely for the puporse of playing (you guessed it) Steel Battalion.
There exist two variants, the first release GREEN button-LED controller (released in 2002) and the BLUE button-LED controller (released in 2004).

## Construction and Details
<img width="600" height="451.5" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c29d35a4-bb24-43db-adf2-58a97a777a5c" />

See [this gallery for more images both external and internal](https://github.com/quizerno/Steel-Battalion-Entry-Guide/blob/main/Archived%20Files/Controller%20Image%20Gallery.md)


* The Left-Center-Right blocks are connected together by IDE cables, which then with brackets are secured with a 3mm hex wrench (61mm long) which can be secured underneath the Center block for safekeeping. 
* The pedal block is connected by a modified PS/2 connector which connects to the Center block. From the Center block an Xbox cable connects to the Xbox,
* Despite its large size, it is not particularly heavy. Weighing only about 17 pounds .
* With the exception of the joystick buttons, every single button on the controller is lit by an LED.
* The pieces from BLUE and GREEN controllers are compatible with each others, and the controller is not region locked.
* Aside from the obvious visual change and different markings, the only mechanic difference between the BLUE and GREEN controllers is the construction of the Pedal Block.
  * On the GREEN controller, the pedal block uses torsion springs. 
  * On the BLUE controller, the pedal block uses compression springs


## Longevity

* The plastic of the original GREEN controller pedals were notoriously prone to breaking.
* The BLUE controller LEDs reportedly burn out easier, but do not affect functionality.
* Both controllers are reported to have some issues where the joystick inputs begin failing either due to pinched wires or loose connections
  * [Left Block Example](https://www.reddit.com/r/steelbattaliononline/comments/1h1piip/left_controller_block_issues/) with culprit being a [loose connector](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c979a250-d1f9-4b16-93ea-f39dc9aaba75).

### Replacement Hardware and Lubricant
* Controller Block Bracket Screws: M4-0.7x8mm, 3mm hex head ([Amazon link](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B08GCGJJFH))
* Underside Hex Wrench: 3mm head x ~61mm length ([Amazon link](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07VKJGGJB))
* Grease (for pedals / sticks / gear shift): Non-petroleum based white lithium grease (basically not a spray can, recommended product is Lucas Oil 10533)

* Button LEDs: 3mm bullet led, forward voltage of 2.7v to 3.5v, brightness of ~ 3000mcd, view angle ~14°, w/ a clear body ([Amazon link](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07QXR5MZB))
* Harcroft Sells replacement Toggle Switch boards (using DSK1033 Phono Line LEVER Switches) and Gear Shifters
